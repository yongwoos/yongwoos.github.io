# 스칼라의 함수의 편미분: 그라디언트
$$ \nabla V = \left( \frac{\partial V}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial V}{\partial y}, \frac{\partial V}{\partial z} \right) $$

# 벡터장의 편미분: 야코비안
$$ \nabla \mathbf{F} = \begin{pmatrix}
\frac{\partial F_x}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial F_x}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial F_x}{\partial z} \\
\frac{\partial F_y}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial F_y}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial F_y}{\partial z} \\
\frac{\partial F_z}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial F_z}{\partial y} & \frac{\partial F_z}{\partial z}
\end{pmatrix} $$

# 벡터장의 발산: 다이버전스
$$ \nabla \cdot \mathbf{F} = \frac{\partial F_x}{\partial x} + \frac{\partial F_y}{\partial y} + \frac{\partial F_z}{\partial z} $$

# 벡터장의 회전: 컬
$$ \nabla \times \mathbf{F} = \begin{pmatrix}
\frac{\partial F_z}{\partial y} - \frac{\partial F_y}{\partial z} \\
\frac{\partial F_x}{\partial z} - \frac{\partial F_z}{\partial x} \\
\frac{\partial F_y}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial F_x}{\partial y}
\end{pmatrix} $$

# 스토크정리
$$ \int_S (\nabla \times \mathbf{F}) \cdot d\mathbf{S} = \oint_C \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r} $$

# 일
$$ W = \int_C \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r} [J] $$
